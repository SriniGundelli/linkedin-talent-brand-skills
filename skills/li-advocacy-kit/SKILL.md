# li-advocacy-kit

When twenty employees paste the same text, the feed treats it as noise and
readers learn to scroll past. When twenty people each say why *they* care, the
reach is real. The kit makes the second easy.

> Also apply the **House rules** section of `~/.claude/linkedin/voice.md`.
> Participation is voluntary. Nothing is posted without each employee's yes.

## Input

The post, announcement or role to amplify: the page link, the facts, the one
sentence that matters, and anything that must not be said (embargo, legal,
numbers not yet public).

## The kit

For each item produce:

**1. Five angles.** Different reasons a person might share it:

| angle | the starter |
| --- | --- |
| Personal | "The part of this I was closest to was ..." |
| Customer | "A customer told us ... and that changed how we built it." |
| Lesson | "What I learned working on this: ..." |
| Context | "Why this matters for {industry people}: ..." |
| Short and plain | "We shipped {thing}. If you {do X}, you will want to see it." |

Each in a short (40 words) and a standard (80 words) form, each with a blank
the person must fill: `{{your reason}}`. The blank is the point. An
unfilled blank stops the post from going out.

**2. A "say it in your own words" prompt.** One question each person answers in
one sentence: "What would you tell a friend who works in {field}?"

**3. Do and don't.** Do tag only people involved, add one personal sentence,
link in the first comment, reply to comments. Don't paste the same post as
colleagues, use the company's hashtags, tag executives for attention, or share
anything marked embargoed.

**4. One message to send** (Slack/email): what it is, why it matters, the five
angles, the voluntary note, and the date. Under 120 words.

## Timing

Stagger. The author or company posts first; employees share across 24-48
hours rather than within the same five minutes. Do not ask for likes or
comments in the share text.

## Rules

- Voluntary, always. No leaderboards, no naming people who did not share, no
  performance tie-in.
- Employees must not state financials, customer names, roadmap or anything
  under embargo. List these explicitly in the message.
- Never write the personal detail for them. Never post from their account.
- Respect local employment and social-media policies.

## Output

The five-angle kit in two lengths, the prompt, the do/don't, the team message,
and a staggered schedule.
