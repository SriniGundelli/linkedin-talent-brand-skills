# Recruiter outreach: the short compliance checklist

This is a prompt for your own judgement, not legal advice. Rules differ by
country, state and employer. Ask your counsel or data-protection lead.

## Data protection (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar)

- **Lawful basis.** Messaging a stranger about a job usually rests on
  "legitimate interests" in Europe and the UK. Keep a short note of why this
  person and why this role.
- **Tell them where you got their details** and what you hold, when you first
  contact them or within a month, as your local rule requires.
- **Honour objections.** If they say stop, stop and record it. Do not message
  from a second account.
- **Minimise.** Keep what you need for the search: name, role, public profile
  link, notes. Do not scrape, and do not store sensitive characteristics.
- **Retention.** Decide a period (many teams use 6-12 months) and delete after.
- **Transfers.** Sending a candidate pack to a client in another country can
  be a restricted transfer. Check before you do it.

## LinkedIn's own rules

- Automated connection or messaging tools and scrapers breach the User
  Agreement and risk restriction of your account. These skills write; you send.
- Respect invitation limits and do not harvest member data from the platform.

## Fair hiring

- Describe the skills the role needs, not the type of person.
- Do not ask about or filter on age, family plans, health, nationality or other
  protected characteristics in outreach or screening notes.
- Record why a candidate progressed or not in job-related terms.

## Confidentiality

- Do not name a confidential client or a candidate's current employer to
  anyone who has no need to know.
- Do not forward a candidate's profile or pay history without their consent.
