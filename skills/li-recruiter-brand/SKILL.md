# li-recruiter-brand

A recruiter's edge is market knowledge: what candidates want, what pay is
doing, why searches stall. Posting that knowledge pulls candidates and hiring
managers in, instead of cold outreach pushing out.

> Also apply the **House rules** section of `~/.claude/linkedin/voice.md`.
> Nothing is posted without the owner's yes. Client and candidate information
> stays anonymised unless named consent is on file.

## Pick a niche first

"Recruiter" is not a position. Use `/li-positioning` if there is no niche yet.
The format is `{type of role} for {type of company} in {geography}`.
Candidates follow specialists.

## Six post types that fit

| type | what it is | the rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market read | what you are seeing across searches this month | one pattern, one number, one implication |
| Pay and terms | ranges, notice periods, counter-offers, equity | source the numbers; state sample size |
| Hiring-manager lesson | a mistake that kills searches | anonymised, constructive |
| Candidate help | CV, interview and negotiation advice | one specific tactic |
| Search post | a live role, framed as a problem | link in the first comment, range stated |
| Behind the desk | how you actually run a search | real, a little imperfect |

## The week (about two hours)

```
Mon  market read or pay post    (from your own data this week)
Wed  candidate help or lesson
Fri  a live search, framed as a problem
Daily  ten minutes: comment thoughtfully on posts by hiring managers and
       candidates in your niche (use /li-comment)
```

## Where the data comes from

Only from what the user supplies: their placements, offer ranges, time-to-fill,
decline reasons, counter-offer rates. Never invent a market statistic. Every
number is labelled with sample size and time period ("across 14 searches since
June"). If the sample is small, say so; it is more credible, not less.

## Compliance and trust

- Anonymise every client and candidate. Change identifying details, and never
  name a confidential client or a candidate's employer.
- Do not publish ranges that identify one person's offer.
- Say when you are an agency or in-house, and who you represent.
- Do not post candidates' rejection stories without permission.
- No automated engagement or scraping.

## Output

Positioning line, the post-type mix for four weeks, a posting calendar, three
drafts built from the user's real data, and the data you still need.
